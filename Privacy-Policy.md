## In short

Phiru has no server of its own, shows no ads and does not track you (no tracking, no analytics tools). What you record in Phiru stays on your iPhone. Data only leaves the device when you explicitly trigger it: when saving to Apple Health, when posting to Strava, or when saving and sharing a picture.

## What data Phiru processes

### On your device

- **Runs:** date, duration, running time, distance, route (if turned on), your feeling and your note.
- **Goals and milestones** that you create.
- **Messages in the mailbox** that someone leaves for you, and the code for it.
- **Settings** such as name, run days and reminder time.

Phiru stores this data only locally on your iPhone. Like all app data, it can be part of your iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on. Apple is responsible for that. If you delete Phiru, the data on the device is deleted.

The controller does not receive any of this data.

### Location

Phiru uses your location only during a run, also in the background with the screen locked. This way voice cues arrive on time and auto-pause notices when you stand still. Route and distance are stored only if “Record route” is turned on; otherwise the location is only processed briefly in memory and not stored. On the share picture, Phiru leaves out the first and last 200 meters of the route so it doesn't show where you live. You grant and withdraw permission in the iOS settings.

### Apple Health

With your permission, Phiru saves every run as a workout (duration, distance, route) in Apple Health. Phiru does not read any health data from Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising and is not passed on to third parties, unless you post a run to Strava yourself. You change the permission in the Health app.

### Strava

If you connect Phiru to your Strava account and tap “Post to Strava”, your iPhone sends the run directly to Strava: title, description, start time, duration, distance and, if available, the route with timestamps. The recipient is Strava, Inc., USA. From then on, Strava’s privacy policy applies. The transfer to the USA takes place at your request (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR).

For the connection, Phiru stores the credentials of your Strava API application and the sign-in tokens exclusively in your iPhone’s Keychain. You can disconnect at any time in Phiru under “More”.

### Photos

At your request, Phiru may add a picture of your run to your Photos. Phiru cannot view your photos.

### Notifications

Your iPhone schedules reminders locally. There is no server and no push service.

## Legal bases

- Storage on the device and the app’s features: performance of the use of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Location, Apple Health, Photos, notifications and Strava: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give via the iOS prompts and by tapping, and which you can withdraw at any time.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the controller does not receive any of your data, you can exercise most rights directly on your device: delete runs in the journal, withdraw permissions in the iOS settings, or delete the app. For data held by Strava, please contact Strava.

## A note on health

Phiru is a training companion and not a medical device. Phiru does not replace medical advice. If you have complaints or pre-existing conditions, talk to your doctor before you start.

## Plans, paces and distances

Training plans and paces are suggestions. You decide yourself how fast and how far you run. Distance and pace are measured by GPS and can differ from the actual distance.

## Liability

Phiru is provided free of charge. Liability is limited to intent and gross negligence, as far as permitted by law.
